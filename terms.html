5. How ordering works

  1. Browse. Customers can browse Shops by area (Central, North, North-East, East or West) and category, view products, and save favourite Shops.
  2. Order. Add items to your cart, choose one of the Shop's pickup time options, and place your order. The order is a request to buy from the Shop.
  3. Order status. Orders move through these stages:
  4. Placed: you have sent the order to the Shop.
  5. Ready: the Shop has marked the order as ready to collect.
  6. Picked up: the order has been collected.
  7. Pickup only. There is no delivery. You collect your order in person at the Shop's pickup address. The Shop's full pickup address, including its unit number, is shown on your order once the order is ready.
  8. Chat. Customers and Shops can message each other in the App about orders.

Prices, stock levels and availability are set by each Shop and can change. A listing is not a guarantee that an item is available.
